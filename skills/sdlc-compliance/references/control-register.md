# Control and evidence register

Use this for an assessment spanning several requirements or owners. Extend the
project's existing register when available; do not create a parallel compliance
system. Keep evidence in its authorized source and record only approved locators.

## Assessment context

Record the product/process and environment, jurisdiction, organizational role,
framework/instrument and version, relevant effective dates, assessed revision or
period, source retrieval date, and explicit inclusions/exclusions. Mark unresolved
applicability questions separately from implementation gaps.

## Per-requirement record

| Field | Useful content |
| --- | --- |
| Requirement ID | Stable source section or a local ID linked to it |
| Authority and applicability | Legal, voluntary, contractual, or internal; exact source and why it applies |
| Expected outcome | Checkable obligation/control outcome, preserving important conditions and exceptions |
| Control location | Responsible code, configuration, or process and relevant environment |
| Evidence | Approved locator, observed fact, revision/period, collection date, and limitations |
| Status | Present, partial, missing, or unknown; scope-specific rationale |
| Gap and impact | Unsupported obligation or unavailable evidence and its practical consequence |
| Owner and next action | Known responsible owner or owner-needed; bounded action and dependencies |
| Verification | Method, actual result, environment/sample/period, and what remains unverified |
| Revalidation trigger | Source/version/date, applicability, code/configuration, process, or scope change |

## Interpret the record carefully

- A disabled control is not present merely because its implementation exists.
- Missing access to evidence usually means unknown; it does not prove the control
  is missing. Conversely, a missing required record can itself be an evidenced gap.
- A sample supports the sampled environment and period; do not extend it silently
  to every customer, system, or operating period.
- Separate the requirement text from engineering interpretation. Preserve the exact
  citation and necessary short excerpt; avoid copying whole proprietary standards.
- Keep legal decisions and accepted exceptions attributable to their actual owner.
  Do not manufacture approval, waive requirements, or mark interpretation settled.
- A status change should point to new evidence or a corrected applicability decision.
  Preserve material unresolved gaps until the receiving owner has assessed them.
